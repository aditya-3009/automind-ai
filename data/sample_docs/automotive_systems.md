## Anti-lock braking system

An anti-lock braking system (ABS) is an electronic system designed to help prevent
wheel lock during braking. ABS-related terminology may appear alongside wheel-speed
sensors, control modules, and warning indicators. Exact behavior and warning meanings
vary by vehicle.

## Electric vehicle battery management

A battery management system (BMS) monitors battery-related measurements such as cell
voltage and temperature, and helps keep a battery pack within its defined operating
limits. Vehicles differ in monitoring strategies, fault codes, limits, and service procedures.

## Driver assistance systems

Advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS) can combine information from cameras, radar,
ultrasonic sensors, and other components. Sensor availability, calibration requirements,
environmental constraints, and supported features depend on the vehicle design.

## Warning indicators and fault codes

A dashboard warning indicator and a diagnostic trouble code are not always enough to
determine a root cause. Meaning depends on the vehicle, the exact code, accompanying
symptoms, and manufacturer documentation. A retrieval system should cite its source
and acknowledge when its corpus does not answer a question.
